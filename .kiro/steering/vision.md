# DesignForYou: Vision

This file says what the product is meant to become. The specs say what is built, step by step.

## Product
DesignForYou is for two groups of people:
- Homeowners who have taste but no CAD skills.
- Designers, who link or upload a portfolio.

Entering a project goes: home page, then portfolio, then projects. Each project has its own setup pages. The app has a dark mode and a light mode. It should look professional and high-end, and it should not feel like a list of tick boxes.

## Inputs
- Inspiration images and notes. First for the whole project, then per room.
- Real measurements or a floor plan upload. Or start from zero with rough templates.

## Items
- Items come from a categorised list with drop-downs: construction, exterior, interior, furniture, lighting, decor. Each category has its own items per room.
- The user gives an image or a description. The app proposes about 4 options. The user picks one. It becomes a 3D model. It can be saved to a library.
- Every list always includes a "custom" choice.

## The design team
Specialist agents work in dependency order:
1. Walls
2. Doors and windows
3. Floors and ceilings
4. Lighting and electrical
5. Furniture
6. Decor
7. Style
8. Reviewer

- A change to an earlier agent's work (for example moving a wall) triggers a re-check of everything that depends on it.
- Agents always ask the user to confirm. They suggest options, and they show two options when they are unsure.
- Locked and Unsure are only a minor, optional tag on an item. They are not a core feature.

## Rules first
- Use knowledge files and fixed rules wherever possible.
- A paid AI model is used only for understanding photos and for taste judgement, and always with a spending cap.

## Honesty
- The screen says clearly what is rule-based and what is AI.
- Plans are drafts for professional review. They are never claimed as verified engineering.

## Knowledge files
- Written in our own words, from open sources only.
- Never copy paid or copyrighted course material into the repo.
